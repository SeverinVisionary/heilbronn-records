## What this branch contains

### Exact baseline and completed narrow results

- `incumbent.py` implements exact arithmetic in the incumbent cubic field,
  verifies all 220 triangle areas, the 20 active triangles, their `8+8+4` D4
  orbit structure, and exact comparison against rational candidates.
- `decimal_verifier.py` independently reconstructs the same coordinates and
  areas with `Decimal` arithmetic.
- `n11_insertion.py` exhaustively proves the Goldberg `n=11` configuration plus
  one arbitrary inserted point reaches only `1/54`; see
  [N11_INSERTION_RESULT.md](N11_INSERTION_RESULT.md).
- `d4_interval_certificate.py` gives a complete exact bracket only for its
  two-parameter D4 incidence family; see
  [D4_INTERVAL_CERTIFICATE_2026-08-16.md](D4_INTERVAL_CERTIFICATE_2026-08-16.md).
- [TRANSVERSAL_NO_GO.md](TRANSVERSAL_NO_GO.md) proves that moving any three or
  fewer labelled incumbent points cannot improve the record.
- `tangent_certificate.py` gives an exact first-order local-isolation
  certificate at the incumbent; it says nothing about remote configurations.

### Numerical and restricted-family discovery tracks

- `frozen_boundary_search.py`, `transversal_search.py`, and
  `transversal_free_search.py` explore active-hypergraph support strata. No
  exact improvement was found.
- `c4_symmetry_search.py` and `c2_boundary_search.py` deliberately relax parts
  of the incumbent symmetry. Their recorded campaigns found no improvement;
  every saved dyadic snap was an exact non-improvement.
- `c4_interval_certificate.py` is an exact Bernstein engine for the full
  three-orbit C4 family. It completes a deliberately loose engine-calibration
  target, but all useful near-record brackets remain incomplete. See
  [C4_INTERVAL_BRACKET_2026-08-17.md](C4_INTERVAL_BRACKET_2026-08-17.md).
- `global_normal_form_search.py` searches all 19 free coordinates in the
  five-boundary normal form. Four recorded high-budget trials and their exact
  dyadic audits were non-improvements; see
  [GLOBAL_NORMAL_FORM_SEARCH_2026-08-17.md](GLOBAL_NORMAL_FORM_SEARCH_2026-08-17.md).

### Genuinely global infrastructure, still incomplete

- `global_mccormick_relaxation.py` builds an auditable outer relaxation with
  normal-form pins, exact target propagation, product/RLT/McCormick rows, strip
  capacity, and optional joint piecewise envelopes. `highs_milp.py` separates
  solver epigraphs from the true geometric minimum of returned coordinates.
  The relaxation remains materially above the record.
- `global_interval_branch.py` is an exact `Fraction` branch-and-bound cover,
  conditional on the published five-boundary theorem. It uses a rational lower
  enclosure of the record, exact determinant vertex hulls, target/chord
  propagation, aligned and shifted strip capacity, and five two-dimensional
  capacity grids.
- The current breadth-first 1,000-box diagnostic pruned no boxes and left 1,001
  pending. The added `--queue-policy depth` changes traversal only; at a
  40-box midpoint diagnostic it reached depth 34 and made four exact triangle
  prunes, versus zero at breadth-first depth 5. Both runs are `INCOMPLETE` and
  have identical completed-cover semantics.
